# Maxwell Krehbiel

M.S. in Business Analytics (May 2027) and B.B.A. in Finance & Real Estate with a minor in Accounting (May 2026), both from TCU's Neeley School of Business.

I build data systems that turn messy real-world information into decisions: quantitative market research, deal sourcing, geospatial analysis, and operational tools.

## Featured work

- **[thesis_to_target](https://github.com/maxskrehbiel/thesis_to_target)**: investment thesis → private-company universe → entity resolution → log-normal EBITDA probability → evidence-backed, non-obvious acquisition targets. Synthetic data.
- **[lidar_terrain_lab](https://github.com/maxskrehbiel/lidar_terrain_lab)**: USGS LiDAR point clouds → bare-earth terrain, canopy height, drainage, and vegetation acreage by zone, exported to maps and Google Earth.
- **[camera_trap_pipeline](https://github.com/maxskrehbiel/camera_trap_pipeline)**: resumable pipeline that turns thousands of trail-camera photos into a queryable dataset with open detection and species models.
- **[options_density_lab](https://github.com/maxskrehbiel/options_density_lab)**: market-implied probability distributions from option prices via Breeden–Litzenberger, with no-arbitrage checks. Synthetic option chains.
